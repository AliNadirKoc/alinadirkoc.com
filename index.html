STR-01 · Featured

Batt2Life

Second-life EV batteries for solar storage · Hult MIB · team of 6 · 2022

EV batteries are replaced once they drop to about 80% capacity, which will leave a growing supply of discarded packs. Batt2Life tests and reconfigures them into affordable storage for homes and communities running on solar, and sends the packs that fail to raw-material recycling.

Trend matrix and research plan, an analysis of South Africa's market and regulations, a 4P/5C marketing plan, and a B2B entry outline with automotive suppliers in Bursa and İzmir.

Target prices are from the team's 2022 business plan. Battery costs have fallen since, so the same packs would likely be priced lower today.
